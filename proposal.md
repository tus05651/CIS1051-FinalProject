## In a paragraph or more, detail your project. What will your software do? What features will it have? How will it be executed?

I plan to use Pygame to build a computer simulated version of blackjack. The program will allow the user to place a bet based on a preset starting amount, and then begin a game. The user will be able to hit or stand based on the cards they are dealt. If they are dealt cards totaling more than 21, the game ends. If and when the user decieds to stand, the dealer's cards will be revealed. If the user wins, their winnings will be added to their bank. If they lose, it will be subtracted. As long as the player's bank is positive, they will be allowed the option to play another round. Once they have lost all of their currency, or if they choose not to continue playing, the game will end.
